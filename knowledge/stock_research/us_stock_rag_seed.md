# US Stock RAG Seed Knowledge

## Pullback Entry Discipline

For swing trading in liquid US stocks and ETFs, a pullback setup is stronger
when price remains above a rising 50-day moving average, holds a visible support
zone, and offers a reward/risk ratio above 2.0 before position sizing is
considered. A stock that is extended far above support should be treated as a
chasing risk unless there is a new base, breakout retest, or clear volatility
compression.

## Earnings And Event Risk

Earnings, guidance updates, regulatory actions, product launches, litigation,
index inclusion, analyst downgrades, and macro rate decisions can invalidate a
pure technical setup. When a catalyst is close, position size should be reduced
or the trade should wait for post-event price confirmation. Stored research
should be used as background only; fresh news and current market data remain
authoritative.

## Fundamental Quality Signals

Useful fundamental context for a stock includes revenue growth trend, gross and
operating margins, free cash flow, debt load, dilution, valuation multiples
relative to growth, and whether management guidance is improving or weakening.
For ETFs, sector concentration, interest-rate sensitivity, commodity exposure,
and liquidity are more important than single-company financial statements.

## Risk Review Checklist

Before a buy plan advances, confirm that the symbol is in the user subscription
list, the market type is allowed, no leverage or options exposure is used, a
stop-loss and invalidation condition exist, position weight is inside the
persona limits, and portfolio drawdown rules are respected. Historical RAG
lessons can challenge a plan but cannot override hard risk gates.
